🌲 Distinct Regions & Topography

New Jersey is home to some of the most varied golf landscapes in America:

  • Pine Barrens: Sandy terrain and pine forests created Pine Valley, consistently ranked the #1 golf course on planet Earth.
  • Urban Skyline Golf: Courses along the Hudson River like Liberty National offer vistas directly facing Lady Liberty and Manhattan.
  • Coastal Linksland: South Jersey features deep dune structures and tidal marshes reminiscent of classic Scottish courses.
